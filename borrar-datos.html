Delete your data from NAOS

Last updated: October 29, 2026.

If you commented on a NAOS post on Instagram or YouTube, NAOS keeps a small record so Cella, its AI assistant, does not reply twice: a hash of the comment and of its author, the date, a category and Cella's reply. It keeps no usernames, photos or comment text (privacy policy).

How to ask for deletion

  1. Write to n1.selfdetermination@gmail.com with the subject "NAOS data deletion".
  2. Include the link to the post and, if you want it gone too, ask for Cella's reply to be deleted.
  3. Within 30 days NAOS deletes the record of your comments and Cella's replies to them, and writes back to confirm.

You can also delete your own comment on Instagram or YouTube at any time, and remove the NAOS app from Instagram under Settings, Website permissions, Apps and websites.

En español

Si comentaste en una publicación de NAOS y quieres que se borre lo que NAOS guarda de ese comentario, escribe a n1.selfdetermination@gmail.com con el asunto "NAOS borrar datos" y el enlace de la publicación. En 30 días como máximo se borra el registro y se te confirma por correo.
