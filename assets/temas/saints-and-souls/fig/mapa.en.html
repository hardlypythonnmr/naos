Nine pieces, 27 Oct to 6 Nov

Details: what this shows, and the list with links

The first two pieces are about the living, the next five about the dead, then one about both, and the last about the dead again. Last quarter falls on Sunday 1 November. The piece you came from has a ring. The moon wanes from nearly full on 27 October to a thin crescent on 6 November.

  2. Thu 29 · Call again next week (about the living)
  3. Sat 31, morning · Three calendars of remembering (about the dead)
  4. Sat 31, night · The dead in three calendars (about the dead)
  5. Sun 1, evening · Saints and Souls (about the dead)
  6. Sun 1, night · Say one name out loud (about the dead)
  7. Mon 2 · The day after the saints (about the dead)
  8. Wed 4 · Set a place for the one you miss (about both)
  9. Fri 6 · Three Masses in one day (about the dead)
